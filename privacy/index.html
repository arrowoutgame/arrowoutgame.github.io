Arrow Out

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026 · Last updated: November 6, 2026
In short: Arrow Out is a puzzle game that runs on your device. You do not need an account, your game progress stays on your phone, and we do not run our own servers that collect your data. The game is free and shows optional rewarded video ads; our advertising partner Google collects limited device data to deliver those ads, as described in Section 4.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the Arrow Out Android game (“Arrow Out”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information. It applies to the app distributed on Google Play under the package name com.arrowout.game. By using the app you agree to this Policy.

2. What the app does on your device

Arrow Out is a casual puzzle game: tap the arrows to slide them off the board and clear each level. You can earn in-game coins and items, and you can choose to watch rewarded video ads in exchange for in-game rewards.

  • No account required. The app gives you a randomly generated player name (such as “Player1234”) that exists only on your device.
  • Game data stays on the device. Your level progress, in-game coins and items, and settings (sound, vibration, effects and language) are stored locally on your device, together with a random installation identifier and a local activity log used by the game logic (for example, levels played and ads watched). We do not upload them.
  • Watching ads is your choice. Rewarded ads are only shown when you tap a button to watch one.

4. Information collected by third parties

Arrow Out is free and supported by ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, and privacy consent in certain regions is managed with Google’s User Messaging Platform. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may automatically collect information such as:

  • Your device’s advertising ID (Google Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it (country or city level)
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, time zone, network type, app version
  • Ad interaction data: which ads were shown, whether they were clicked or completed, and related diagnostics

Google uses this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure performance, prevent fraud and, where permitted, show ads tailored to your interests. We request ads that are suitable for a general teen-and-older audience. This collection is governed by Google’s own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

We do not receive information from Google that identifies you personally. We may receive aggregated reports (such as the number of ad impressions and revenue) that do not identify individual users.

Game engine

Arrow Out is built with the Unity game engine. When the app starts, Unity may automatically collect limited, non-personal technical information about your device, such as device model, operating system version and hardware specifications, to improve its engine. This collection is governed by the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Your advertising choices

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path may vary by device).
  • If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

5. Information we collect ourselves

We do not require an account and do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts or photos through the app. We do not use our own analytics, crash reporting or tracking servers.

If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and the content of your message. We use it only to respond to you and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose.

The game data described in Section 2 stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

6. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before our advertising partner uses your data for personalized advertising, as required by the GDPR, the ePrivacy Directive and Google’s EU User Consent Policy. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy options in the app’s Settings. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Because we do not hold personal data about you on our own systems, most requests about ad data should be directed to Google; we are happy to help you with that.

7. Users in the United States

We do not sell personal information for money. Under some US state laws (such as the California Consumer Privacy Act), allowing advertising partners to collect identifiers for targeted advertising may be considered “selling” or “sharing” personal information. You can opt out at any time by opting out of personalized ads or deleting your advertising ID as described in Section 4.

8. Children’s privacy

Arrow Out is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data retention and security

Since your game data never leaves your device, we do not retain it. Information collected by our advertising partner is retained according to its policies. We take reasonable measures to protect the limited information we handle, such as emails you send us, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
